Everyday Makeup Tips

  1. Clean and moisturize your skin.
  2. Apply foundation in thin layers.
  3. Blend your blush carefully.
  4. Apply mascara from roots to tips.
  5. Finish with lipstick or lip gloss.

Things to Avoid
